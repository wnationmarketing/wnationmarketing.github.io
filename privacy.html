WnationMarketing ("WnationMarketing," "we," "us," or "our") operates online product-content and marketing activities, including applications that interact with third-party platforms such as Pinterest.

Our Pinterest application is designed for use by WnationMarketing for its own business activities. We do not operate a service that requires members of the public to create accounts with us.

Depending on how the application is used, information processed may include Pinterest account information made available through the Pinterest API, such as account or board identifiers required to publish Pins.

We do not intentionally collect Pinterest passwords.

2. Pinterest API

Our application may use the Pinterest API to authenticate an authorized Pinterest account, access permitted boards, and create Pins on authorized boards.

Pinterest information is accessed only through the permissions granted during the Pinterest authorization process and is used for the functionality described above.

The application may process publicly available product information, product images, product titles, product descriptions, and product URLs from retailers in order to create product-related Pinterest content.

Pinterest Pins may contain affiliate destination links. These links may redirect users to participating retailers or affiliate services.

The WnationMarketing automation application may store information locally on the computer on which the application is installed. This may include product-processing records, generated content, application settings, and authentication information required for authorized API access.

Local application data is not intentionally sold or provided to third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Third-Party Services

The application may interact with third-party services including Pinterest, Google services used for spreadsheet management, retailer websites, affiliate services, and locally installed AI software.

Information handled by those services may also be subject to their respective privacy policies and terms.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect credentials and application data used by the application. Authentication credentials and tokens are intended to be stored securely and are not intentionally published publicly.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate, troubleshoot, maintain, and improve the application and its associated business processes.

8. Children's Privacy

Our application is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, the updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Questions regarding this Privacy Policy may be directed to WnationMarketing through the contact information associated with the business or application.
